Home Screen Cleanup

Adds "Remove from Continue Watching" and "Remove from Next Up" to the "..." menu of cards in those two home-screen rows of the web client. Removing from Continue Watching resets the saved playback position of the item (and all of its versions) to the start; its played state, play count and last-played date are not changed. Because of that, a reset episode can afterwards appear in Next Up. Removing from Next Up hides the whole series from that user's Next Up list on every client until it is unhidden here or, if enabled, until the user plays an episode of that series again.

Adds the plugin's script to the web client page. Turn off to remove the menu entries from the web client entirely; the Next Up filter keeps working. Takes effect when the web client is reloaded.
When a user starts playing any episode of a series they hid from Next Up, the series is unhidden for that user.
